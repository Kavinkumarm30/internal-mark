📖 Usage Instructions

Purpose: This calculator computes internal marks based on CAT exams and assignments using the exact formula specified below.

Formula Explanation:

CAT1 = (((C1 / 100) × 60) + A1) / 100 × 20
CAT2 = (((C2 / 100) × 60) + A2) / 100 × 20
TOTAL = CAT1 + CAT2

Where:

  • C1, C2 = CAT exam marks (0-100)
  • A1, A2 = Assignment marks (0-40)
  • Each CAT score is out of 20 marks
  • Total internal score is out of 40 marks

How to Use:

  • Enter marks in the input fields (validates automatically)
  • Click "Calculate CAT-1" to calculate CAT-1 score
  • Click "Calculate CAT-2" to calculate CAT-2 score
  • Click "Calculate Total" to calculate the combined total score
  • Toggle "Show rounded integers" to switch between decimal and rounded display
  • Save student records with their name to track multiple students (Staff only)
  • Export all saved students to CSV for external use (Staff only)
  • Import CSV files - format: Name,C1,A1,C2,A2 (Staff only)

CSV Format (Staff only):
